What to remember

  • Data pipelines move records and evidence; agents interpret and act within boundaries.
  • ADPL is the shared blueprint: adpl, meta, pipeline, agents, ahi, and summary.
  • AHIL is typed, bidirectional, and append-only: agents surface findings, humans issue directives.
  • Approvals, overrides, and acknowledgements create an auditable chain instead of silently changing history.
